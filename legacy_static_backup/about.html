About Me

Hello! I'm Sahil R. Pathan, a final-year B.Tech Computer Science student at DBATU University, Maharashtra, India.

I specialize in front-end and back-end web development with hands-on experience in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Angular, React, Node.js, Express, Java, and Spring Boot, with databases like MySQL and MongoDB.

I'm always learning, always building, and always looking for ways to push the boundaries of what's possible with code.
